About Kyle

I'm Kyle. I live in Newmarket and run Lloyd's Local Services myself. If you message the business, you're talking to me. If I take the job, I'm the one who shows up.

Before LLS, I spent eight years commercial fishing in New England, including offshore lobster, gillnet, and groundfish dragger work. It was hands-on work around equipment, long days, and problems that had to be figured out with what you had.

That background carries over well to what I do now: understand the problem, use the right tools, and get the job handled without making it more complicated than it needs to be.

I don't pretend to do work that belongs with a licensed trade. If that's what you need, I'll tell you. I can still help figure out who you need, find the right person, coordinate the work, and handle the parts that are in my lane.
