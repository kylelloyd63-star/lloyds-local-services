Privacy notice

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This notice covers lloydslocalservices.com and its quote form. It's run by Kyle Lloyd, Lloyd's Local Services, out of Newmarket, NH. The SourceToMe and SourceInk apps have their own policies: SourceToMe privacy policy and SourceInk privacy policy.

Browsing the site

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads, or tracking scripts. It's hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Cloudflare provides the site's DNS and forwards email sent to the business address.

What the quote form collects

  • Your name
  • Your phone number or email address, so I can reply
  • Your town
  • Your description of what you need help with
  • Any photos you choose to attach (optional, 10 MB total)

Please don't include passwords, account numbers, or other sensitive information.

How the form works

When you press Send, your browser sends the form directly to FormSubmit, a form service run by Devro LABS. FormSubmit may ask you to complete a Google reCAPTCHA check to block spam, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to that check. FormSubmit then emails your request to kyle@lloydslocalservices.com, which Cloudflare forwards to my inbox.

FormSubmit's documentation says it keeps form submissions for 30 days and does not keep uploaded files. FormSubmit's own terms are in its privacy policy. You can contact them at support@formsubmit.co.

How I use your information

I use what you send only to reply to you, give you a quote, and do the work you ask for. I don't sell it, share it for marketing, or add you to a mailing list. I won't publish any photo you send, or a photo of your home or equipment, without your permission.

How long I keep it

I keep what you send only as long as I need it to reply, give you a quote, do the work, and keep basic business records. You can ask me to delete it.

Your choices

You can ask me what I have from you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email kyle@dripstaticstudios.com and I'll get back to you.

Payments

This website doesn't take payments or collect card details. Invoices are sent to you directly.

Children

This site and its services are meant for adults. I don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this notice changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
