SourceInk privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

SourceInk is a handwriting diary app for Windows made by Lloyd's Local Services LLC. This page covers SourceInk 1.5.0 and explains what happens to what you write and draw.

Your diary stays on your PC

Your pages, drawings, settings and notes are saved only on your PC, in your Windows user folder (%LocalAppData%\Inkwell). When SourceInk is installed from the Microsoft Store, Windows keeps this folder inside the app's own data folder. We don't run any server that receives your writing. SourceInk has no account, no cloud sync, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

The diary works fully without AI. AI replies are optional and stay off until you choose how you want them.

Sealed storage

If you use your own API key, it's stored encrypted with Windows protected storage (DPAPI), so only your Windows account can read it. We never see your key.

Pages are stored as plain files unless you turn on the optional “sealed to you” setting. You'll find it on the back page under PAGES ON DISK, and it's offered while the Windows Hello lock is on. With it on, each page and drawing saved on disk is encrypted with DPAPI for your Windows account. Export always writes plain files, so keep exported files somewhere safe.

Where your words go when SourceInk writes back

You choose where AI replies come from. Before anything is sent, SourceInk shows a “Sends to” line naming where it will go.

  • Local mode (Ollama on this PC). Your page goes to Ollama, a free program running on your own computer, and stays on your PC. If you change the Ollama address under “advanced” to another computer, the “Sends to” line says so, and your page goes to that computer instead.
  • Key mode (a service you pick). You can enter a key for an AI service, for example OpenAI, Google, Groq or one you type in. SourceInk then sends that service the text of the page you ask it to answer, or a PNG picture of your drawing in Draw mode. So that the diary can remember, your recent pages and its replies are sent along with it, and the preview tells you how many. That service's own privacy policy then applies, and it may charge you for use.

In key mode you see a preview of exactly what will be sent before it goes, with three choices:

  • Send once asks again next time.
  • Always allow stops asking for that one service only. Any other service asks again.
  • Use local instead switches to Ollama on your PC.

Press Esc to send nothing. To stop sending at any time, switch to the local AI or turn AI replies off in “connect a brain”.

Nothing else connects

Apart from the AI you choose, SourceInk only opens web pages in your browser when you click a link, such as the Ollama download page or a service's key page. There is also a developer-only link to a tool on the developer's own PC. It stays off unless someone places a set-up file by hand, and the app never sets it up.

Purchases

SourceInk is free to download. An optional one-time $9.99 unlock, where offered, is sold only through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles the payment under its own terms and privacy statement. We don't receive your card or payment details, only the Store's confirmation that your Windows account owns the unlock. SourceInk keeps that confirmation (encrypted for your Windows account) and a count of your free AI replies in its settings on your PC, so the unlock works offline. Neither is sent to us.

AI replies can be wrong

Replies are written by a language model. They can be inaccurate or upsetting, and they are not professional, medical or crisis advice.

Reporting a reply

You can report any reply, either with “report” under the reply or with “report the last reply” on the back of the diary. A report is saved on your PC. It only reaches us if you choose “save and email it to us”. That opens your own email app with the report filled in, so you can read it before you send it. We use emailed reports only to make replies safer, and we'll delete one if you ask.

Tester notes

Tester notes are saved on your PC and are never sent anywhere.

Forget and removal

Forget erases a page from your diary on your PC. Uninstalling the Microsoft Store version also removes its data folder, so export first if you want to keep your pages. If you installed a copy another way, uninstall it and then delete the %LocalAppData%\Inkwell folder if it's still there.

Children

SourceInk isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
